# Routing

Public exports are `findRoute`, `generateInstructions`, and `polylineLength`; shared types come from `@wayfinding/map-engine`.

`findRoute` uses zero-heuristic A* (Dijkstra) so vertical transitions, zero-cost links, and calibrated mixed units cannot cause an overestimating heuristic. Equal-cost paths follow graph insertion order deterministically. Only confirmed nodes and edges with `restriction: 'open'` participate. A confirmed subset may route even when the aggregate graph status is candidate or unknown; graph status describes overall coverage and never promotes an unconfirmed link. Accessible routes additionally require `accessibility: 'confirmed'` and exclude stairs. Room directory visibility does not authorize navigation.

Graph `distance` values are authoritative nonnegative costs, including vertical travel not represented by a 2D polyline. A confirmed finite positive `metersPerUnit` converts map-unit costs to meters and permits meter costs in the same search. Without confirmed calibration, every usable weight must declare map units. Mixed uncalibrated units, invalid weights, duplicate IDs, invalid coordinates, missing endpoints, disconnected endpoint geometry, and nonvertical links between floors produce a bilingual `invalid` result. Ineligible candidate or closed links never participate. `polylineLength` returns drawing-unit length, zero for empty/single-point input, and `NaN` for nonfinite points.

Every eligible edge is checked against supplied `walls` and `navigableAreas`. Any wall contact (crossing, touching, or collinear overlap) fails closed; a door opening must be a gap in the wall segments. Each full polyline segment must lie in the union of the supplied navigable polygons. Validation partitions the segment at every polygon-boundary intersection and checks each interval, so endpoints and a single midpoint cannot hide excursions through a concavity or a gap between polygons. Navigable boundaries count as contained within a drawing-coordinate tolerance of 1e-7; this is not a physical clearance claim. Invalid constraints fail closed. Same-node routes also require a safe location.

Same-floor edges must declare their endpoints' floor. Vertical edges must declare one endpoint's floor. The v1 graph cannot associate obstacles or polygons with individual floors, so a graph containing multiple confirmed floors plus any supplied geometry constraints returns `invalid`: projecting vertical travel onto a floor's enclosure would invent validation. Empty constraint arrays remain supported, including isolated synthetic multi-floor fixtures, but provide no independent enclosure/wall verification. Source confirmation, constraint completeness, public access and accessibility evidence remain the ingestion/semantic pipeline's responsibility. No synthetic graph ships as application data.

Instructions orient each polyline to the direction traveled. In SVG coordinates (x right, y down), clockwise turns are right turns. Absolute turns below 15 degrees continue straight, from 15 to less than 45 degrees are slight, and 45 degrees and above are full turns. Internal bends are preserved; their distance is null because the contract provides a whole-edge travel cost. A vertical connection announces its destination floor and resets facing direction. Side-of-destination instructions require a final door edge and a destination door/room node. `generateInstructions` rejects noncontiguous, forbidden, or unconfirmed supplied paths with an empty array.

Validation: `npx vitest run tests/unit/routing.test.ts tests/unit/instructions.test.ts tests/integration/route-safety.qa.test.ts` (64 passing tests, including four unchanged independent QA regressions). All test maps in `tests/fixtures/routing.ts` are explicitly synthetic, unrelated to B03.
